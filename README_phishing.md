# CodeAlpha_PhishingAwareness

A phishing awareness training presentation, built for the CodeAlpha Cyber Security internship (Task 2).

## What it covers

1. **What phishing is** and why it works
2. **4 red flags in a phishing email**: urgent tone, suspicious sender, generic greeting and mistakes, unexpected links or files
3. **Spotting fake websites**: look-alike domains such as `paypa1.com` and `google.com.verify-login.net`
4. **Social engineering tactics**: urgency, authority, fear, reward and curiosity, plus smishing, vishing and spear phishing
5. **Real-world examples**: the Google/Facebook invoice fraud (2013-2015), the 2016 campaign email leak, and the 2020 Twitter phone attack
6. **Best practices**: think before clicking, multi-factor authentication, password managers, verifying requests through a second channel, reporting suspicious messages
7. **Quick quiz**: 3 questions with answers to test what was learned

## How to use it

Present the slides in order. On the quiz slide, read each question aloud and let the audience answer before revealing the answer.

## What I learned

- How to recognise the common signs of phishing emails and fake websites
- How attackers use psychological pressure to make people act without thinking
- Simple habits that stop most phishing attacks
